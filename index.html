How it works

When someone cuts us off or criticises our work, we often blame, defend, deny or justify before we’ve had a chance to choose. The game lets you practise catching that moment.

The line of choice

Stay above the line

Above it is personal responsibility: owning how you respond. Below it are the automatic reactions. Dropping below is normal; the aim is to notice and come back up.

The quarter second

Triggers pause the game

Everyday moments, like a teammate who hasn’t done their part, freeze time. Choose your response before autopilot chooses for you.

Emotional health levels

Climb from 5 to 1

You start at Level 5, Automated Response, where most of us live. XP moves you up through Recognition, Social Value and Wisdom to Presence.

Personal responsibility is never blame. Nobody is responsible for becoming unwell. We can take responsibility for how we respond, and that includes asking for help.

Line of Choice is based on Global Leadership Foundation’s guide to emotional health and leadership, and is part of the Emotional Health Project workshops.

Stay above the line

You start at Level 5, where most of us live: plenty of automatic responses. Run, earn XP, and climb the emotional health levels to Level 1, Presence.

  • Above the red line you earn 5× more XP. Dropping below is normal; staying there drains your presence.
  • Dodge the clutter (pings, to-dos, doomscrolls) by switching sides: ↑ ↓, swipe, or tap the top or bottom of the track.
  • Triggers freeze time for the magic quarter second. Pick the response that takes personal responsibility with 1 2 or a tap. Wait too long and your automatic response picks for you.
  • Collect head, heart and body orbs. All three = Presence flow: 2× XP and clutter can’t touch you.
  • Inner observer O slows the world for 3 seconds.
